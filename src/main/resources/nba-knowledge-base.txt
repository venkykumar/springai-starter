NBA KNOWLEDGE BASE
==================

SCORING RECORDS AND MILESTONES

LeBron James became the NBA's all-time leading scorer on February 7, 2023, surpassing Kareem Abdul-Jabbar's record of 38,387 points. LeBron achieved this milestone with the Los Angeles Lakers against the Oklahoma City Thunder. As of the 2024-25 season, LeBron James has scored over 40,000 career points, making him the only player in NBA history to reach that mark.

Kareem Abdul-Jabbar held the NBA all-time scoring record for 39 years (1984-2023). He scored 38,387 career points across his time with the Milwaukee Bucks and Los Angeles Lakers. Kareem is also the inventor of the sky hook, considered one of the most unstoppable shots in basketball history.

Wilt Chamberlain scored 100 points in a single game on March 2, 1962, playing for the Philadelphia Warriors against the New York Knicks. This remains the highest single-game scoring total in NBA history and is widely considered one of the most unbreakable records in sports. Chamberlain also averaged 50.4 points per game during the 1961-62 season, another record that still stands.

Michael Jordan holds the record for the highest career scoring average in NBA history at 30.1 points per game. Jordan played primarily for the Chicago Bulls and later the Washington Wizards. He won six NBA championships, all with the Chicago Bulls, and was named Finals MVP all six times.

THREE-POINT RECORDS

Stephen Curry holds the NBA record for the most career three-pointers made. He surpassed Ray Allen's record of 2,973 three-pointers on December 14, 2021, against the New York Knicks at Madison Square Garden. As of 2025, Curry has made over 3,700 career three-pointers, a record that continues to grow.

Stephen Curry set the single-season three-point record in 2015-16 by making 402 three-pointers, breaking his own previous record of 286 set in 2012-13. This 402 three-pointer season came during the Golden State Warriors' 73-9 regular season record.

Ray Allen previously held the career three-point record before Curry surpassed him. Allen made 2,973 career three-pointers and was renowned for his shooting stroke and work ethic. He famously hit the tying three-pointer in Game 6 of the 2013 NBA Finals for the Miami Heat.

Klay Thompson holds the record for the most three-pointers made in a single game, hitting 14 three-pointers against the Chicago Bulls on October 29, 2018.

GOLDEN STATE WARRIORS

The Golden State Warriors are an NBA franchise based in San Francisco, California, currently playing at the Chase Center. The team won NBA championships in 2015, 2017, 2018, and 2022. Their core group of Stephen Curry, Klay Thompson, and Draymond Green, often called the "Splash Brothers" (Curry and Thompson for shooting), formed one of the most dominant teams in NBA history.

The 2015-16 Golden State Warriors finished with a 73-9 regular season record, the best in NBA history at the time, surpassing the 1995-96 Chicago Bulls' record of 72-10. Despite this record, they lost the NBA Finals to the Cleveland Cavaliers in seven games.

Stephen Curry, born March 14, 1988, is the son of former NBA player Dell Curry. Steph plays for the Golden State Warriors and is a two-time NBA MVP (2015, 2016), with the 2016 MVP being unanimous. He is widely considered the greatest shooter in NBA history and revolutionized the game with his long-range three-point shooting.

Draymond Green is a four-time NBA champion with the Golden State Warriors known for his defensive versatility, playmaking, and ability to guard multiple positions. He was the NBA Defensive Player of the Year in 2017. Green is known for his energy, rebounding, and ability to facilitate offense from the forward position.

Klay Thompson is a five-time NBA All-Star and four-time champion with the Golden State Warriors, known for his quick-release shooting and elite perimeter defense. Thompson missed the 2019-20 and 2020-21 seasons due to serious knee and Achilles injuries before returning to help Golden State win the 2022 championship.

LOS ANGELES LAKERS

The Los Angeles Lakers are one of the most storied franchises in NBA history, having won 17 NBA championships. Notable championship teams include those led by Jerry West and Wilt Chamberlain in 1972, the "Showtime" Lakers featuring Magic Johnson and Kareem Abdul-Jabbar (1980, 1982, 1985, 1987, 1988), the three-peat Lakers with Shaquille O'Neal and Kobe Bryant (2000, 2001, 2002), the 2010 championship again with Kobe Bryant and Pau Gasol, and the 2020 bubble championship with LeBron James and Anthony Davis.

Kobe Bryant played all 20 seasons of his NBA career with the Los Angeles Lakers. He scored 33,643 career points (fourth all-time) and won five NBA championships. Bryant scored 81 points against the Toronto Raptors on January 22, 2006, the second-highest single-game total in NBA history. He was an 18-time All-Star and two-time Finals MVP. Tragically, Bryant passed away on January 26, 2020.

Magic Johnson played point guard for the Los Angeles Lakers from 1979 to 1991 and briefly in 1996. He won five NBA championships and three MVP awards. Johnson was known for his exceptional court vision, passing ability, and playing the point guard position at 6'9". He and Larry Bird are credited with reviving interest in the NBA in the 1980s.

Shaquille O'Neal played for the Los Angeles Lakers from 1996 to 2004, winning three consecutive championships (2000, 2001, 2002) alongside Kobe Bryant. O'Neal is considered one of the most dominant players in NBA history. He was a three-time Finals MVP, four-time champion, and 15-time All-Star.

BOSTON CELTICS

The Boston Celtics are the most decorated franchise in NBA history with 18 championships. Under coach Red Auerbach and center Bill Russell, the Celtics won 11 championships in 13 seasons from 1957 to 1969, including eight consecutive titles from 1959 to 1966. The Celtics won their 18th championship in 2024, defeating the Dallas Mavericks.

Bill Russell won 11 NBA championships in 13 seasons with the Boston Celtics, the most by any player in NBA history. Russell is widely considered the greatest winner in NBA history and was also an exceptional defender and shot blocker. He was the first Black head coach in major North American professional sports when he became player-coach of the Celtics in 1966.

Larry Bird played for the Boston Celtics from 1979 to 1992, winning three NBA championships (1981, 1984, 1986) and three consecutive MVP awards (1984, 1985, 1986). Bird is considered one of the greatest forwards and shooters in NBA history. His rivalry with Magic Johnson elevated the NBA's popularity in the 1980s.

Paul Pierce spent 15 seasons with the Boston Celtics and was the 2008 NBA Finals MVP when Boston won the championship. He later played for several other teams but is most associated with the Celtics and had his number 34 retired by the franchise.

Jayson Tatum is the current star of the Boston Celtics, winning the 2024 NBA championship and being named Finals MVP. Tatum is known for his scoring versatility, clutch performances, and multiple All-Star selections.

CHICAGO BULLS

The Chicago Bulls won six NBA championships, all in the 1990s with Michael Jordan: 1991, 1992, 1993, 1996, 1997, and 1998. The team accomplished two three-peats under coach Phil Jackson. Their 1995-96 team went 72-10, setting what was then the best regular season record in NBA history.

Michael Jordan, widely considered the greatest basketball player of all time, played for the Chicago Bulls from 1984 to 1993 and from 1995 to 1998. He also played for the Washington Wizards from 2001 to 2003. Jordan won six NBA championships, six Finals MVP awards, five regular season MVP awards, and was a 14-time All-Star.

Scottie Pippen was Michael Jordan's sidekick on the Chicago Bulls dynasty teams. A seven-time All-Star, Pippen won six championships with the Bulls and is considered one of the greatest defensive forwards in NBA history. He was also part of the 1992 US Olympic "Dream Team."

Dennis Rodman, known as "The Worm," played for the Chicago Bulls from 1995 to 1998 and was a key part of their second three-peat. Rodman won five NBA championships overall (two with Detroit Pistons, three with Chicago Bulls) and led the league in rebounding a record seven consecutive seasons.

MIAMI HEAT

The Miami Heat were founded in 1988 and have won three NBA championships: 2006, 2012, and 2013. In 2006, they beat the Dallas Mavericks with Dwyane Wade winning Finals MVP. LeBron James joined the Heat in 2010 alongside Dwyane Wade and Chris Bosh, forming the "Big Three" that won back-to-back championships in 2012 and 2013.

Dwyane Wade played most of his career with the Miami Heat and is considered one of the greatest shooting guards in NBA history. Wade won three championships (2006, 2012, 2013) and was a 13-time All-Star. He won the 2006 Finals MVP after averaging 34.7 points per game.

Jimmy Butler joined the Miami Heat in 2019 and quickly became the face of the franchise. Known for his work ethic, clutch performances, and defensive intensity, Butler helped lead the Heat to the NBA Finals in 2020 and 2023.

SAN ANTONIO SPURS

The San Antonio Spurs won five NBA championships: 1999, 2003, 2005, 2007, and 2014, all under head coach Gregg Popovich. Tim Duncan was the cornerstone of all five championship teams. The Spurs are known for their team-oriented system, player development, and organizational stability.

Tim Duncan is widely considered the greatest power forward in NBA history. He played all 19 seasons of his career with the San Antonio Spurs, winning five championships. Duncan was a two-time MVP, three-time Finals MVP, and 15-time All-Star. Known as "The Big Fundamental," he excelled at the fundamentals of basketball.

Tony Parker was a four-time NBA champion with the San Antonio Spurs and the 2007 Finals MVP. A native of France, Parker was a dynamic point guard known for his mid-range game, speed, and ability to navigate defenses. He was the first European player to win Finals MVP.

Manu Ginobili, from Argentina, played 16 seasons for the San Antonio Spurs and won four championships. He was known for his creativity, Euro step move, and ability to perform in big moments. Ginobili won the 2005 Sixth Man of the Year award and was inducted into the Hall of Fame in 2022.

OKLAHOMA CITY THUNDER AND KEVIN DURANT

Kevin Durant won two NBA championships with the Golden State Warriors in 2017 and 2018, winning Finals MVP both times. Durant previously played for the Seattle SuperSonics/Oklahoma City Thunder, where he won the NBA scoring title four times and the MVP award in 2014. Durant is considered one of the most skilled scorers in NBA history at 7 feet tall.

Russell Westbrook played for the Oklahoma City Thunder alongside Kevin Durant and won the MVP award in 2016-17 after averaging a historic triple-double of 31.6 points, 10.7 rebounds, and 10.4 assists per game. Westbrook set the record for most career triple-doubles, surpassing Oscar Robertson's previous record of 181.

MILWAUKEE BUCKS

The Milwaukee Bucks won two NBA championships: 1971 with Kareem Abdul-Jabbar and Oscar Robertson, and 2021 with Giannis Antetokounmpo. The 2021 championship ended a 50-year title drought for Milwaukee.

Giannis Antetokounmpo, nicknamed "The Greek Freak," was born in Athens, Greece, to Nigerian immigrant parents. He joined the Bucks in 2013 and won back-to-back MVP awards in 2019 and 2020. In 2021, he led the Bucks to the NBA championship and won Finals MVP, averaging 35.2 points per game in the Finals. He is known for his unique combination of size (6'11"), speed, and skill.

DENVER NUGGETS

The Denver Nuggets won their first NBA championship in 2023, defeating the Miami Heat. Nikola Jokic was the Finals MVP after averaging 30.2 points, 14.0 rebounds, and 7.2 assists per game in the Finals.

Nikola Jokic, born in Sombor, Serbia, has won three MVP awards (2021, 2022, 2024) and is considered by many to be the most skilled center in NBA history. At 7 feet tall, Jokic plays as a point center, averaging over 10 assists per game in multiple seasons. He is known for his passing, court vision, and unique post moves.

NBA DRAFT AND HISTORY

The NBA was founded in 1946 as the Basketball Association of America (BAA) and merged with the National Basketball League (NBL) in 1949 to form the NBA. The league initially had 17 teams.

The NBA Draft takes place annually, typically in late June. Teams select players in reverse order of the previous season's standings, with the worst teams receiving the highest picks through the NBA Draft Lottery. The lottery system was introduced in 1985.

Magic Johnson was selected first overall by the Los Angeles Lakers in the 1979 NBA Draft. LeBron James was selected first overall by the Cleveland Cavaliers in the 2003 NBA Draft. Stephen Curry was selected seventh overall by the Golden State Warriors in the 2009 NBA Draft.

NBA RULES AND GAMEPLAY

A standard NBA game consists of four 12-minute quarters. Games are played on a court 94 feet long and 50 feet wide. The shot clock is 24 seconds, meaning teams must attempt a shot within 24 seconds of gaining possession. The three-point line is 23 feet 9 inches from the basket at the top of the arc and 22 feet in the corners.

A team commits a foul when a defensive player makes illegal contact with an offensive player. After a team accumulates 5 team fouls in a quarter, the opposing team shoots free throws on subsequent fouls. Players are disqualified after committing 6 personal fouls.

The NBA season runs from October through June. The regular season consists of 82 games per team. The playoffs begin in April with 16 teams (8 from each conference). The NBA Finals series is best-of-seven.

The NBA All-Star Game takes place in February. The game features the best players from the Eastern and Western Conferences. The format has changed over the years; currently teams are drafted by two All-Star captains.

NOTABLE NBA RECORDS

Oscar Robertson averaged a triple-double for the entire 1961-62 season (30.8 points, 11.4 rebounds, 11.4 assists). No player had accomplished this feat again until Russell Westbrook in 2016-17.

Jerry West is the player depicted in the NBA logo. West played for the Los Angeles Lakers from 1960 to 1974 and won one NBA championship in 1972. He was an excellent scorer and defender, known as "Mr. Clutch."

The single-game assist record of 30 is held by Scott Skiles, set on December 30, 1990, while playing for the Orlando Magic against the Denver Nuggets.

The NBA record for most rebounds in a single game was set by Wilt Chamberlain with 55 rebounds against the Boston Celtics on November 24, 1960.

John Stockton holds the career records for both assists (15,806) and steals (3,265). Stockton played his entire career with the Utah Jazz alongside Karl Malone, forming one of the greatest duos in NBA history. Despite their individual success, Stockton and Malone never won an NBA championship.

HALL OF FAME PLAYERS

Magic Johnson, Larry Bird, Michael Jordan, Wilt Chamberlain, Bill Russell, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Shaquille O'Neal, Kobe Bryant, Tim Duncan, and LeBron James are all members of the Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame and are consistently ranked among the greatest players of all time.

Dirk Nowitzki played 21 seasons with the Dallas Mavericks, winning the NBA championship in 2011 and Finals MVP. Nowitzki was the first European player to win the NBA MVP award (2007) and is considered the greatest European player in NBA history. He finished third in career scoring with 31,560 points.
